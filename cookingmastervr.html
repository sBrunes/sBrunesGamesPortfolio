Cooking Master VR
Upper Limb Therapy
Overview
This is a physical therapy VR application created for the purpose of conducting research with Traumatic Brain Injury (TBI) patients. In this immersive experience, the patient is guided by a physical therapist to complete one of two potential recipes. In addition to this, the activity is able to completed in 3 enviornments with varying visual complexity (simple/standard/complex), in order to measure the affects of visual complexity on cognitive load.
During my time on this project, I programmed and designed interactions and overall flow. This required frequent collaboration with development team members and collaborators, to ensure the features of the application were meeting research requirements. I also contributed to training Physical Therapy students in the operation and procedure of using the application.
