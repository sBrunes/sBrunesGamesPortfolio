POCUS Carnival
Educational Mobile Game
Overview
This is a project that I worked on during my time as an intern for Indiana University eLearning Design and Services. I was given the task of co-designing an educational mobile game with a client from the IU School of Medicine. The goal was to create a simple mobile game that could be played by first-year medical students to get them interested in learning about ultrasound concepts.
The project was created with the intent to be playable on both mobile phones, and on a browser. Throughout the project, I would frequently meet with stakeholders to get feedback about developoment, and receive direction. I was also responsible for directly interacting with target players via focus groups, and 1 on 1 interviews. Throughout the duration of the project, I spoke with over 150 individual students for the purpose of playtesting, and receiving feedback about their experience with the game.
Through this process, several significant changes to the overall game structure were made based on the feedback I received, which resulted in the design reaching a measurably improved state from where it began.
