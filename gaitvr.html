Gait VR
Physical Therapy for TBI patients
Overview
In this project, I wanted to gain get more experience developing gameplay features for a multiplayer game. To do this, I spent a couple months prototyping a simple 1v1 competitive helicopter fighting game where you control all 3 axis of rotation for your own helicopter. In the game, you fire sticky balls of tar at your opponent. When the tar balls stick to the other player's helicopter, they make the helicopter more difficult to control.
During my time prototyping, I got more experience working with things like replication, and peer-to-peer networking. While this project was ultimately just a prototype and excuse for me to improve my skills, I implemented several gameplay features.
