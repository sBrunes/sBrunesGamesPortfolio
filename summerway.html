Summerway
Horror Game Prototype
Overview
This is a small horror game prototype that I made. In this game, you move around a snowy 50-level parking garage while being stalked by a scary monster! While trying to find your car, you must seek shelter in small outposts to avoid snow storms, and track generator locations using a radio.
2022
