Potion Master VR
Upper Limb Therapy Prototype
Overview
This is a personal project prototype that I created to demonstrate a more fantastical scenario / task for Upper Limb focused Therapy VR. The topic surrounding benifits of mirroring real-life activities (like cooking, folding laundry, etc.) in Therapy VR applications has been thoroughly discussed. While investigating research around this topic, I thought it would be interesting to make a prototype that essentially replicates specific real-world activities (in this case, cooking / cutting things), and dresses it up in a more interesting environment.
